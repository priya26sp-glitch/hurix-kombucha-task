In this task, I present an analysis of multi-batch fermentation kinetics and biochemical profiles of kombucha tea produced using recycled agro-industrial by-products (tea dust and blackstrap molasses) compared to traditional substrates (black tea and sucrose).
Time-course measurements spanning a 12-day fermentation period are stored in /app/data/fermentation_time_course.csv. The dataset includes daily records of pH, acetic acid concentration, lactic acid concentration, total polyphenols, tannins, flavonoids, and antioxidant activity (measured via DPPH and FRAP assays) across multiple replicates.
The objective is to perform data cleaning to handle missing or noisy sensor records, compute reaction rate kinetics, execute analysis of variance (ANOVA) to evaluate the significance of substrate substitution on metabolite accumulation, and validate antioxidant capacity trends against biochemical expectations.
Write all final kinetic parameters and model coefficients to /app/results/optimized_kinetics.json, and generate a statistical summary table of significant metabolite differences at /app/results/metabolite_anova_summary.csv.
